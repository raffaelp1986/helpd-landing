helpd Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

1. Who we are

helpd LLC ("helpd", "we") runs the helpd app and helpd.me for people in the United States. Contact: helpd@helpd.me, 4473 Spruce Ln, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33309. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and share it, and your choices. It is meant to serve as our notice at collection for California residents under the CCPA, as amended by the CPRA.

2. Information we collect

Account information: name and email address; whether you use helpd to give, to receive, or both; and your sign-in method. You create an account with your email address (a magic link or a password) or with Sign in with Apple. Sign-in is handled by Supabase, which also provides our database and file storage for bill photos. If you sign in with email and a password, the password is stored by Supabase and is not readable by us. If you use Sign in with Apple, we receive your name and email address as Apple shares them with us, which may be a private relay address Apple provides instead of your real email.

Request and profile information (Recipients): first name and last initial, city, bill category, bill amount and due date, your story, your sponsorship reason, and any story photos you choose to add.

Bill documents (Recipients): a photo of your bill and, for rent, your lease and landlord statement. These can contain your full name, address, account numbers, and sometimes financial details.

Payment information: card payments are collected and processed by Stripe. We receive a Stripe customer ID, card brand and last four digits, payment status, and amounts. We do not receive or store full card numbers.

Recipient identity and bank information: collected directly by Stripe when you set up payouts (legal name, date of birth, address, ID document, Social Security or tax ID number, bank account). We receive your Stripe account ID, verification status, and payout information as Stripe shares them with us.

Sponsorship records: the amount, recipient, term, dates, and your consent to each Sponsorship.

Communications and reports: messages you send us, reports you file, and our replies.

Device and app data: basic technical data needed to run the app and keep it secure, such as app version and error logs. helpd uses no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools.

Camera and photo library: only when you choose to upload a photo.

We do not collect precise location. City is typed in by Recipients.

3. How we use information

To create and secure accounts; to review Requests and sponsorship reasons and prevent fraud; to process payments, payouts, refunds, and disputes through Stripe; to run Sponsorships; to show Requests and profiles to other users; to send receipts, reminders, and service messages; to respond to reports and support requests; to meet legal obligations; and to improve the safety and reliability of the Service. We use bill documents only to review and verify Requests, handle disputes, and meet legal duties. We do not use bill documents for advertising.

4. How we share information

With other users: first name and last initial, city, category, amounts, story, sponsorship reason, and story photos on a live Request or profile. Contributors can choose to hide their name from Recipients and public pages. We do not show your full bill.

With Stripe: payment, payout, and identity information needed to process payments and verify Recipients. Stripe's use is governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy.

With Supabase: account authentication, our database, and file storage for bill photos and other documents you upload. Supabase's use is governed by Supabase's Privacy Policy.

With Apple: if you use Sign in with Apple, Apple provides your name and email address (which may be a private relay address) and processes that sign-in under Apple's Privacy Policy.

With Amazon Web Services (AWS): AWS hosts the Supabase project that stores our authentication, database, and file storage.

With Google: the app loads fonts from Google Fonts, which can expose your device's IP address and user agent to Google. Google's use is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

With other service providers that host and run helpd, such as email delivery providers, under contracts that limit their use of your information.

With reviewers: helpd staff and contractors who review bill documents and sponsorship reasons.

For legal and safety reasons: to comply with law, respond to legal requests, enforce our Terms, and protect people from fraud or harm.

In a business transfer such as a merger or sale, with notice to you.

helpd does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Sensitive information

Bill documents, stories and sponsorship reasons can reveal sensitive details such as financial hardship, and Recipient identity numbers are handled by Stripe. We use sensitive personal information only to provide and secure the Service and for the purposes described above. Please cover details that are not needed, such as full account numbers, before you upload or write.

6. How long we keep information

Account information: while your account is open, then deleted or anonymized within 30 days after deletion, except as below.

Bill documents, leases, and landlord statements: deleted 90 days after a Request closes (funded, expired, cancelled by you, or removed) or is rejected, so that fraud claims and card disputes can be handled. We keep them longer only when they are needed for an open claim or dispute or a legal obligation. Payment records are kept as the law requires.

Story text, sponsorship reason, and photos: removed when the Request or profile is removed or your account is deleted.

Payment, payout, refund, dispute, tax, and identity records: kept as long as the law or Stripe's rules require, typically seven (7) years.

Sponsorship consent records: kept for at least three years, or one year after the Sponsorship ends, whichever is longer.

Reports and fraud records: kept as needed for safety and legal reasons.

7. Deleting your account and your choices

You can delete your account in the app: Settings > Delete account. We will close your open Requests, cancel your Sponsorships, delete your profile, saved cards, and story photos, and delete or anonymize your other information, except records we must keep as described above. You can also email helpd@helpd.me. You can change notification settings in the app. You can hide your name on Contributions.

8. California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you live in California, then to the extent applicable you may ask us to: tell you what personal information we collect, use, and disclose (right to know); delete your personal information, subject to legal exceptions; correct inaccurate information; and limit our use of sensitive personal information. We do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of. We will not treat you differently for using these rights. To make a request, use Settings > Privacy, or email helpd@helpd.me. We will verify your identity, usually by matching information to your account, and respond within 45 days. An authorized agent may make a request for you with your written permission.

Categories collected in the last 12 months: identifiers (name, email); commercial information (contributions, sponsorships); financial information (limited Stripe data); internet or device information (basic app data); audio, electronic, or visual information (photos you upload); sensitive personal information (bill documents that may reveal financial details; Recipient government ID and account details are collected by Stripe). Sources: you, Stripe, Apple (if you use Sign in with Apple), and your device. Purposes: as described in Section 3. Disclosed to: Stripe, Supabase, Amazon Web Services, Apple, Google, other service providers, and other users as described in Section 4.

9. Children

helpd is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we will delete it. If you believe a child has used helpd, email helpd@helpd.me.

10. Security

We use access controls, encryption in transit, private storage for bill documents, and limited staff access. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you we will notify you as the law requires.

11. Where we store information

We and our service providers, including Supabase, store personal information in the United States (Oregon), via Supabase on Amazon Web Services. The Service is offered only to people in the United States.

12. Changes and contact

We will post updates here and tell you in the app for material changes. Contact: helpd LLC, 4473 Spruce Ln, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33309, helpd@helpd.me.
